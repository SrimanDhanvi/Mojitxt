About The MojiText Chat

Simple, Sustainable, Social.

Our Philosophy

MojiText is a real-time emoji-only wall where anyone can instantly express feelings without accounts, usernames or history. Messages appear for a few seconds and then fade away forever, creating a living stream of pure emotion. No text, no pressure, just raw mood and presence.

We prioritize simplicity in design and function, making it easy for anyone to jump in and start chatting instantly, without complicated sign-ups or confusing settings.
