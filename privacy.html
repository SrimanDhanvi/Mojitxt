Privacy Policy

For MojiText Users

1. Introduction

This is the official Privacy Policy for **MojiText**. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. We believe that a great user experience should not come at the cost of your personal data. Our goal is to be transparent about what data we interact with and why.

2. What Data We Collect

MojiText does not require personal accounts or registration. We do not directly collect, store, or require any personally identifiable information (like your name, email, or physical address).

However, for essential security, functionality, and performance monitoring, our backend provider, **Firebase**, automatically collects certain non-personal data when you access the website. This may include your **IP address**, general **device information** (e.g., browser type), and access times. This information is used solely to protect the service against abuse and ensure stability.

3. Third-Party Services

To operate and monetize MojiText, we rely on the following trusted third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Provides the backend infrastructure, hosting, and database services. They handle the essential security and operational data mentioned in Section 2.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable and necessary steps to protect your anonymity and the integrity of the MojiText service. Because we do not store personal accounts, our focus is on operational security.

Crucially, all messages within the MojiText application are temporary and are **not stored permanently** on our servers. They are automatically cleaned up shortly after being sent.
